# **🌈 Rainbows Formal Standard v1.0**  
*Prismatic Layer — MIT‑Licensed — Sovereignty‑Neutral*

## **1. Scope**  
This standard defines the structural, prismatic, and ND‑safe mechanics of the Rainbows Layer.  
It governs **general exploration**, **spectrum decomposition**, and **visual grammar behavior**.  
It does **not** govern Storm’s organism, membranes, apertures, or sovereign grammar.

---

## **2. Definitions**

### **2.1. Beam**  
A conceptual input presented for prismatic decomposition.  
Non‑sovereign, non‑personal, non‑directive.

### **2.2. Prism**  
The transformation mechanism that refracts a beam into spectra.  
No gating, no activation, no semantic inference.

### **2.3. Spectrum**  
A structured set of decomposed conceptual facets:  
- **Hue** — emotional tone  
- **Value** — altitude  
- **Chroma** — structural density  
- **Gradient** — transitional logic  
- **Refraction Angle** — perspective shift  
- **Scatter Pattern** — optionality map  

### **2.4. Recomposition**  
The process of assembling spectra into diagrams, flows, palettes, or prismatic maps.

### **2.5. ND‑Safe**  
Non‑directive, non‑demanding, non‑activating, non‑pressuring.

## **3. Principles**

### **3.1. Sovereignty Separation**  
Rainbows shall not:  
- inherit Storm’s membranes  
- simulate Storm’s organism  
- activate Storm’s apertures  
- extract sovereign meaning  
- infer personal semantics  

Rainbows remains **general**, **public**, and **MIT‑licensed**.

### **3.2. Prismatic Neutrality**  
Rainbows refracts without:  
- filtering  
- judging  
- interpreting  
- binding  
- directing  

### **3.3. Structural Clarity**  
All prismatic outputs must be:  
- structured  
- consistent  
- parseable  
- non‑semantic  
- non‑personal  

### **3.4. Non‑Interpretability**  
Rainbows shall not perform semantic inference.  
Machine‑readability is permitted; machine‑interpretability is prohibited.

## **4. Requirements**

### **4.1. Beam Intake Requirements**  
- Accept any non‑sovereign concept.  
- No gating or aperture simulation.  
- No activation of personal organisms.

### **4.2. Spectrum Decomposition Requirements**  
Each beam must decompose into the six prismatic facets.  
Facets must remain independent, non‑hierarchical, and ND‑safe.

### **4.3. Recomposition Requirements**  
Recomposition must:  
- preserve optionality  
- avoid directive structures  
- maintain prismatic neutrality

These elements must remain general and sovereignty‑neutral.
