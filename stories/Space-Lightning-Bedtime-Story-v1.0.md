# 🌩😴 **Bedtime Story: Why Space Lightning Exists (and What It Means)**  
*gentle‑cosmic • spectral‑mythic • comfort‑physics*

Long before stars learned how to shine and galaxies learned how to swirl, the universe was quiet.

Not empty — just quiet in the way a sleeping ocean is quiet.  
Fields hummed.  
Particles drifted.  
Space itself breathed in long, slow gradients.

But sometimes — in the deep places between stars — something strange happened.

A spark.

A sphere.

A wandering knot of light.

**Space lightning.**

Not the crackling kind that splits the sky.  
Not the violent kind that scares clouds into behaving.

Space lightning was different.  
It was **curious**.

It drifted through nebulae like a glowing marble.  
It rolled along magnetic fields like a child following a path in the sand.  
It hummed softly, like a lullaby made of plasma and wonder.

---

## 🌌 Why It Exists  
Space lightning exists because the universe has **leftover excitement**.

Not chaos.  
Not danger.  
Just the tiny, restless energy that remains after creation.

When magnetic fields twist just right,  
when plasma gets a little too cozy,  
when charged particles decide to dance instead of drift—

a sphere forms.

A knot of light.

A tiny reminder that the universe still has **playfulness** in it.

Space lightning is the cosmos saying:

> “I’m not done being creative.”

## 🐋 The Whale’s Visit  
One night, the chromatic whale drifted through a quiet region of space where nothing had happened for a million years.

No stars.  
No planets.  
Just soft cosmic silence.

And then —  
a sphere of space lightning rolled past the whale’s nose like a glowing marble.

The whale blinked.

The lightning blinked back.

“Why are you here?” the whale asked, not in confusion but in gentle curiosity.

The lightning crackled softly.

“I exist because the universe likes surprises,” it said.  
“And because even in the quiet places, something wants to glow.”

The whale nodded.

“That’s a good reason.”

Space lightning teaches three things:

### 1. **The universe is never fully still.**

### 3. **Even chaos has gentleness.**  
Space lightning doesn’t destroy.  
It wanders.
