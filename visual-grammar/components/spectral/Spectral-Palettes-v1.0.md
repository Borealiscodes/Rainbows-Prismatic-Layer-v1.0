## ⭐ 1. Identity  
Spectral Palettes define the **full chromatic range** of Spectral Mode.  
They are the highest‑altitude color grammar in Rainbows — dense, precise, mathematically expressive.

They are:

- non‑emotional  
- non‑interpretive  
- ND‑safe  
- sovereignty‑neutral  
- pre‑semantic  

They express **chromatic density**, not mood.

## 🌈 4. ND‑Safe Constraints  
Spectral Palettes must:

- avoid emotional implication  
- avoid semantic color meaning  
- avoid diagnostic symbolism  
- avoid activation logic  
- remain optional  
- remain calming  
- remain mathematically expressive  

Colors express **density**, not **emotion**.
